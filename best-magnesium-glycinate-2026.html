What to Look For in a Magnesium Glycinate Supplement

Not all magnesium glycinate is created equal. Here's what separates the good from the garbage:

  • Elemental magnesium content: The label might say "magnesium glycinate 1000mg" but that's the weight of the entire chelate. What matters is the elemental magnesium — usually 100-200mg per serving.
  • Pure glycinate vs. blends: Some products mix glycinate with cheaper oxide to boost the elemental number. Check the "other ingredients" section.
  • Third-party testing: Look for USP, NSF, or independent lab verification. This confirms the product actually contains what it claims.
  • Filler ingredients: Magnesium stearate, silicon dioxide, and rice flour are common. They're generally safe but some people prefer to avoid them.
  • Serving size: If you need 4 capsules per serving, that bottle of 180 caps only lasts 45 days. Do the math.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best time to take magnesium glycinate?

Most people get the best results taking magnesium glycinate 30-60 minutes before bed. The glycine component has calming properties that support sleep. However, it can be taken any time of day with or without food.

How much magnesium glycinate should I take daily?

The recommended daily allowance for magnesium is 310-420mg for adults. Most supplements provide 100-200mg of elemental magnesium per serving. Start with one serving and increase if needed, but don't exceed 400mg of elemental magnesium without consulting a healthcare provider.

Can magnesium glycinate cause diarrhea?

Magnesium glycinate is the least likely form to cause digestive issues. Unlike magnesium citrate or oxide, the glycinate form is gentle on the stomach and rarely causes diarrhea even at higher doses.

What's the difference between magnesium glycinate and bisglycinate?

They're essentially the same thing. Bisglycinate means two glycine molecules are attached to each magnesium atom, which is the standard chelation for this form. Some brands use "glycinate" and others use "bisglycinate" but the product is identical.

Is magnesium glycinate safe to take every day?

Yes, magnesium glycinate is safe for daily use at recommended doses. It's one of the safest forms of supplemental magnesium with minimal side effects. However, if you have kidney disease or take certain medications, consult your doctor first.

Does magnesium glycinate help with anxiety?

Research suggests magnesium supplementation may help reduce anxiety symptoms, particularly in people who are magnesium-deficient. The glycine component also has calming properties. While it's not a replacement for professional treatment, many users report noticeable improvements in anxiety levels.
