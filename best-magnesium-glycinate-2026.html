How We Tested

We evaluated over 30 magnesium glycinate supplements based on five criteria: elemental magnesium content per serving, third-party testing certifications, filler ingredients, capsule/tablet size, and cost per serving. We also considered user reviews across thousands of verified purchases and consulted with a registered dietitian on bioavailability claims.

One thing we noticed immediately: many "magnesium glycinate" products actually contain a blend of glycinate and oxide. We flagged those in our reviews because you're paying a premium for glycinate — you should actually get it.

4. Life Extension Magnesium Caps — Best Blend

★★★★☆ 4.5/5 (6,500+ reviews)
$10–$14 (100 capsules)

Life Extension takes a different approach by combining magnesium glycinate with citrate and oxide in one capsule. The idea is you get the gentle absorption of glycinate plus the higher elemental content of oxide. It's a smart formulation for people who need higher doses without taking a handful of pills.

The trade-off is that it's not pure glycinate — if you specifically need the calming glycine component, you're getting less of it per serving. But for general magnesium supplementation, this is an excellent and affordable choice.
