Why Magnesium Glycinate?

Magnesium glycinate is the gold standard for supplemental magnesium. Unlike cheaper forms like magnesium oxide (which has roughly 4% absorption), glycinate is chelated — meaning the magnesium is bound to the amino acid glycine. This gives you two benefits: superior absorption and virtually zero digestive issues.

Most people choose magnesium glycinate for sleep support, anxiety relief, muscle relaxation, or to address a deficiency without the laxative effect that comes with citrate or oxide forms.

Who Should Take It?

  • Anyone with trouble sleeping or staying asleep
  • People experiencing muscle cramps or tension
  • Those with anxiety or stress-related symptoms
  • Athletes needing recovery support
  • Anyone whose diet is low in magnesium-rich foods
